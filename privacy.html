Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Welcome to Buzzer Companion, developed by BuzzerShowdown-Dev. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

Buzzer Companion does not use advertising, analytics or external tracking services.

The app uses:

  • Camera access to scan QR codes and join games.
  • Player names entered by users.
  • Game information, including buzzer presses and related activity.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the Buzzer Showdown gaming experience. Player names and game activity are communicated to the Buzzer Host.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising companies. Information is shared with the game host to operate the game.

4. Camera Permissions

Camera access is used for QR code scanning. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

5. Data Security

BuzzerShowdown-Dev aims to minimise the information handled by Buzzer Companion.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact BuzzerShowdown-Dev@protonmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Buzzer Companion develops. Any changes will be published on this page.
